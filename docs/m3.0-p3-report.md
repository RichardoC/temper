### Seed scheme — clarification, not deviation

The standing guardrail is "seed_paired_across_temps semantics where ladders
serve multiple temps". At P3's ladder that semantics degenerates, and the
arithmetic forks three ways:

| option | seeds | problem |
|---|---|---|
| **A: FLAT** | `base_seed+i` = [0…7] | **chosen** |
| B: full pairing (`--seed-paired-across-temps`, K=1) | all 8 canvases get **seed 0** | P2 proved the seed path is the only RNG source ⇒ the two T=0.80 canvases are **byte-identical**, likewise the two T=0.95 ones. 2 of 8 canvases per problem are provable duplicates = 25 % of a 14.9 h cell redrawn |
| C: grouped pairing | per-temp seed {0,1} | 8 distinct canvases, but the comparability it preserves is consumed by nobody here (P1's pairing exists to isolate temperature at K=4 *per temp* with a reachability gate; P3's slots are independent configurations with 1–2 canvases per temp) |

Supervisor ruling: **(A) FLAT**. The rev2 doc named *temperatures*, not seeds,
so this is a clarification. FLAT also keeps game 0 bit-anchored to the banked
s256 anchor, making the ship gate's zero-regression check on the 11-passer
cohort **mechanical rather than statistical**.

**Verified on the real canvases, all 25 problems:** every T=0.2/seed-0 sidecar
is byte-identical (sha256) to the corresponding `20261020T100251Z-s256.jsonl.raw`
p0 sidecar — **25/25 identical**.

## PHASE B — scoring window + the two emission tables

Scoring window: model exit **ps-verified (0 temper/dream/hf procs) BEFORE**
Rancher was started; Rancher up in 40 s; the **full 8-canvas ensemble** of all
25 problems container-scored (official + normalized, norepair); Rancher quit and
**verified down** (0 procs, socket gone) before any analysis ran.

Export: **174 completions + 26 exclusions** of 200 candidate rows. Honest
counting folds the 26 in as FAILURES — which is what both emission tables do.

C-null and C-screen are **not two generations**: they are two emission rules
over the same completed ensemble, computed offline from committed sidecars by
`scripts/m3_p3_emissions.py`. Both rules live in `temper/steer/emission.py`, the
same module the in-loop C-full engine calls.

| cell | rule | honest | official |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-null** | T=0.2 canvas if R1-clean, else lowest-T clean | **14/25 (0.56)** | 0.56 |
| **C-screen** | best R1-clean by mean commit confidence, ties lowest T | **14/25 (0.56)** | 0.56 |
| anchor-1024 (banked s256) | — | 11/25 (0.44) | 0.44 |
| anchor-2048 (banked s512) | — | 16/25 (0.64) | 0.64 |

The emission rule **diverged on 15/25 problems but on the same pass count** —
the two rules agree on *whether*, not on *which* canvas.

### Paired migrations (D6, sign-test primary; Jeffreys reporting-only)

**C-null vs anchor-1024** (+3): 11 pass→pass / 0 pass→fail / 3 fail→pass /
11 fail→fail. Flips UP: 17, 6, 9. One-sided sign test **p = 0.1250**.

**C-null vs anchor-2048 (−2)**: 13 pass→pass / **3 pass→fail** / 1 fail→pass /
8 fail→fail. Flips UP: 17. Flips DOWN: **10, 12, 25**. Sign test p = 0.9375.

### Three findings that shape the rest of P3

**1. C-null (14) is BELOW anchor-2048 (16), and the lost problems are exactly
the budget-fixable cohort.** 10, 12 and 25 were all won by the unsteered s512
anchor and all three are lost by the ladder. This is the design's own caveat
materialising: s512 spends 2048 NFE on **four 512-step** canvases (fine commit
grid); the ladder spends the same 2048 on **eight 256-step** canvases (coarser
grid, more draws). The ladder buys *diversity* and pays in *commit
granularity*, and on the budget-fixable cohort granularity was what those
problems needed. The control is therefore **not a free win over the anchors** —
which is precisely what review B's 13–16/25 null bet predicted (measured: 14,
dead centre).

**2. The ladder's single gain over anchor-2048 is HumanEval_17 (+1)**, the
exclusion-pair problem the P1 probe found reachable at T=0.8. Per the
`m3.0-report.md` warning, C-null reproduces it, so **a 17 flip can never count
as C-full mechanism evidence**. The attribution gate's outside-cohort
requirement is live and stricter than the design text assumed.

**3. Selection, not reachability, is the binding constraint.** Among the eight
canvases per problem, **16/25 problems have at least one container-OK canvas**
(the oracle), but **both rules pick only 14**. And they miss *different* ones:

| rule | available but missed |
|---|---|
| C-null | HumanEval_24 (4 OK canvases, emitted a T=0.2 Exception), HumanEval_25 (1 OK, emitted a T=0.95 Exception) |
| C-screen | HumanEval_1 (1 OK at T=0.2, emitted a T=0.8 Exception), HumanEval_9 (2 OK, emitted an export-excluded T=0.95) |

The screen is **not** an oracle and slightly mis-ranks: confidence ranks a
compile-clean-but-semantically-wrong canvas above a compile-clean-and-correct
one. **Two machines already see 16/25; the emission rule forfeits 2.** This is
the single most actionable measurement in P3 for M3.1, and it also means the
primary C-full − C-screen delta is partly a *rule* effect unless C-full's
ensemble coincides with C-null's — recorded as a forensics precondition.
