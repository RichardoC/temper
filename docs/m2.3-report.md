1. **Honest pass@1 is budget-insensitive from 96→128: 0.04 → 0.04** (official
   0.05→0.0435 on differently-sized scored subsets — not comparable; honest is).
   The only passing problem remains HumanEval_23_strlen (4/4 OK in both
   cells); repair-edition delta is 0 in both; edition delta is 0 in both.
2. **The decode-budget disease RELocATED rather than vanished**: at 128 all
   five steps=96 exclusion problems (HumanEval_4/15/17/18/19) extract
   cleanly — adjacent-token corruption that shredded fences/braces/signatures
   at 96 is gone there — but two NEW problems (HumanEval_24_largest_divisor,
   HumanEval_25_factorize, all 8 rows) collapsed the same way (unclosed
   fences, no plausible span). Exclusions 20→8; SyntaxError share of SCORED
   ~constant (0.95→0.96): exclusions converted to scored failures, capability
   unchanged. So "M2.2's 0.04 was a decode-budget artifact" is **not
   supported at 128**; the honest 0.04 is looking like a real capability
   floor on this slice, with corruption-rate roughly conserved per-cell.
3. **Phase-0 instrument fix did its job where it could and is honestly
   attributed**: re-exporting the unchanged 96 pilot with the fenced→bare
   fallback converts 20→16 exclusions (the 4 HumanEval_15 doc-echo rows);
   at 128 the fallback fired on **zero** rows (old extractor would also
   extract 92/100) — the 96→128 exclusion drop is attributable to STEPS, not
   to the fix. The fix remains justified (demonstrated 4-row recovery on the
   96 cell, zero-cost when unneeded). Whether 256 collapses the corruption
   rate (M2.2's forensic prediction) is exactly what the pending cell must
   answer.

## Answer to "was M2.2's 0.04 a decode-budget artifact?"

**No — not at 128.** Three independent readings agree: (a) honest pass@1/4
identical (0.04) across 96→128 with the SAME trivial-only pass set;
(b) SyntaxError share of scored ~constant (0.95→0.96) while exclusions fell
20→8 — the disease converts from "invisible exclusion" to "scored
SyntaxError" rather than to "pass"; (c) the specific 96-cell corruption
signatures (shredded `numbers[i + 1]`, renamed `how_times`, doc-echo fences)
disappear at 128 but the per-cell corruption mass is conserved (new collapses
in 24/25). Decode budget at these levels changes WHERE the model fails, not
HOW OFTEN it fails to produce compiling Rust. Caveat worn openly: one cell
(128) of the planned two-cell ablation is in; 256 could still buck the trend
(M2.2's prediction was that 256 specifically collapses the SyntaxError share
— that hypothesis is UNTESTED, and is the next run's first deliverable).
