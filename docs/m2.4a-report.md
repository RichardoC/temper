# temper — M2.4a report (fourth cell: steps=512 — staircase or cliff top?)

Milestone: **M2.4a** — the fourth cell of the M2.3 steps ablation. Same
everything as 96/128/256 (prompt template sha `bdd6144a…`, seeds base_seed+i,
T=0.2, entropy remasking, canvas 384, K=4, 25 problems, schema v1.2, official
container scoring amd64/Rosetta rustc 1.75.0), except `steps=512`. Ran on the
fixed-shape sampler by default (MPS compile-cache fix `a90df10`;
TEMPER_NATIVE_DREAM_SAMPLE unset — no env flag needed, verified end-to-end by
a 1-canvas pre-flight probe at the exact s512 config before launch:
533.9 s/canvas = 1.043 s/step, no driver growth death).

## Verdict (3 lines)

1. **CLIFF TOP, not staircase: 512 steps is a perfect plateau.** s512 is
   bit-for-bit level-identical to s256 on every scored metric — honest
   pass@1 = pass@4 = 0.16 (official 0.1739), SyntaxErr share 0.83 (76/92),
   the SAME 4 passers (2/7/15/23), the SAME 8 exclusions (17 ×4, 19 ×4),
   repair Δ +0.000, edition Δ 0.000 — at 2× the NFE (2048 vs 1024/problem)
   and 2× the wall (14.48h vs 7.21h). The budget response is a step
   function: 0.04 → 0.04 → 0.16 → 0.16; the threshold lives between 128 and
   256 and 256 is already on the plateau.
2. **Per-problem migration 256→512 is TOTAL STASIS: 0/25 problems moved.**
   4 held-pass, 19 held-fail (every one SyntaxError ×4 at BOTH steps), 2
   held-excluded. The two persistent exclusion problems (17 prose-echo,
   19 adjacent-token corruption `Vec<&'>`) are now stable across TWO
   consecutive cells — the "per-cell lottery" framing from M2.3 is
   superseded: 17/19 are deterministic failures of this prompt shape at
   this temperature, not sampling luck.
3. **Sample diversity collapses further at 512** (same raw_sha256-based
   measure on both cells): 20/25 problems emit byte-identical text across
   all 4 seeds at 512 vs 13/25 at 256 (median distinct texts 1 in both,
   max 3). Unsteered BoN is losing its exploration exactly where the
   capability plateau begins — more NFE now buys nothing and even the
   best-of-K mechanism is starving. What remains (83% compile-dead scored
   rows, the 17/19 prompt-shape exclusions, diversity collapse) is
   precisely the residue that budget cannot reach: it is L1 steering's job.

## Staircase-vs-cliff verdict

**Cliff top (saturation), with the step located between 128 and 256.** The
four-cell curve hon p@1 = 0.04, 0.04, 0.16, 0.16 against NFE/problem = 384,
512, 1024, 2048 is a step function, not a ramp: doubling 96→128 moved
nothing, 128→256 moved 4×, doubling 256→512 moved nothing — not on
aggregates (identical to the third decimal), not per-problem (0/25
migrations), not in failure composition (19/19 held-fail problems are
SyntaxError ×4 at both steps). M2.3's two rejected candidates are now three:
*budget-linear* and *capability floor* were rejected there; *more-steps-
helps-slowly* is rejected here. 512 doubles the cost of the s256 cell and
returns exactly zero. There is no evidence that any steps ∈ (256, ∞) helps
this configuration, and the diversity measurement gives a mechanism: at 512
the per-seed temperature-driven exploration has largely died (20/25
identical), so the sampler is converging to the same fixed point faster than
the extra steps could diversify.

## Canonical B2 recommendation

**Adopt steps=256 (1024 NFE/problem, ~7.2h/cell wall) as the canonical B2
config.** Rationale: 512 is measured to be strictly dominated (identical
scores at 2× NFE and 2× wall); 128 and below sit below the threshold with
the 0.04 artifact; 256 is the cheapest measured cell that sits on the
plateau. This also confirms the M2.4 note's sizing decision to run the
Qwen2.5-Coder-7B twin (B3) at steps=256. Two operational notes carry over:
(a) keep the fixed-shape sampler default (verified leak-free at 512 —
plateau probes at 320 and a full 100-canvas s512 cell at 1.018 s/step);
(b) per-canvas cost at 512 is exactly 2× s256's, so no superlinear penalty
from the MPS fix — but there is no reason to pay it.

## Implications for L1 steering

The ablation series now cleanly partitions the failure mass into what budget
fixed and what only steering can reach:

1. **What budget fixed (and is therefore NOT steering's job):** the
   coarse-commit adjacent-token corruption class below ~256 steps — the
   0.04 artifact, cleared at 256 and holding at 512. Steering experiments
   should use 256 steps as the substrate and treat sub-256 corruption as
   solved-by-budget.
2. **What budget cannot fix (steering's target list):**
   - **Compile-dead scored rows: 76/92 (0.83) at BOTH 256 and 512**, all
     SyntaxError ×4 per problem, stable problem set. These are decode-order
     incoherence failures that more denoise steps do not repair (and L0.5
     repair adds +0.000 in all four cells). Fitness signals already cheap
     at steer time — extractability, must_define, compile — catch exactly
     this mass; at 256, 83% of canvases are dead weight that FK/SMC
     resampling should kill early.
   - **The 17/19 exclusion pair, now provably deterministic** (stable across
     two cells, same failure modes as the phase-0 forensics). 17 is a
     prompt-shape disease (prose echo after the fence) — template A/B with a
     response-format contract is the labeled fix, worth ~+0.04 hon p@1 via
     scoring-fraction alone (92/100 → ~100/100 if 17 falls). 19's
     `Vec<&'>` corruption is a resampling target: a fitness signal that
     rejects `&'>`-shaped spans kills those particles before they waste the
     budget.
   - **Diversity collapse:** 20/25 problems byte-identical across seeds at
     512 (13/25 at 256) means unsteered best-of-K is structurally exhausted
     — there is nothing for BoN to select among. L1's value proposition
     sharpens: inject exploration (temperature/alg cycling across particles,
     resampling from the pooled candidate set) rather than widen K.
3. **Design constant confirmed:** the MPS compile-cache fix is load-bearing
   for L1 (steering runs many more canvases per problem); it is now verified
   stable at 2× the canonical step count with no env flag required.

## Sample-diversity measurement note

Measured by sha256 of raw sidecar texts per (problem, seed) from the
committed records (`raw_sha256` field), same method both cells: s256 distinct
texts per problem min/med/max = 1/1/3 (13/25 problems all-identical); s512 =
1/1/3 (20/25 all-identical); no problem has 4 distinct texts in either cell.
This refines (with a named, reproducible method) M2.3's sidecar caveat, which
reported "2–4 distinct, median 2" for s256 — the ordering conclusion
(T=0.2 collapses seeds; BoN curves must not be extrapolated from K=4) is
unchanged, and the 256→512 delta (13→20 identical) is measured on a single
consistent basis.
